Captain America

The First Avenger

Captain America was the first Avenger, he was known for his missions in Europe

He saved the world when he crashed a plane full of Hydra Bombs and the Tesseract in the freezing ocean.

His Villains

Red Skull, Hydra, Taskmaster and Baron Zemo.

Bonus Information: Captain America was in the ice for over 70 Years his best friend is also James Bucky Barnes
